Research Papers

Plan in 3D, Shoot in One Take: Coherent Multi-Action Long-Take Video Generation in Interactive Scenes

Keyue Xing*, Hongkun Lyu*, Jiayi Gao, Zhu Xu, Yang Liu

Under review, CVPR 2027 · Video generation

Ariadne enables continuous multi-action one-take video generation by coordinating character motion, camera paths, and evolving scene states on an updatable 3D stage.

Self-Evolving Agents for Context-Aware Video Editing (EditEvolve)

Jiayi Gao, Keyue Xing, Changcheng Hua, Yang Liu

Under review, ICLR 2027 · Video editing

EditEvolve lets editing systems adapt to varied video contexts by reusing feedback-verified tool workflows, without retraining the underlying models.

DuplexAct-Bench: Broadening Full-Duplex Speech Evaluation toward Proactive Interaction across Diverse Behavioral Requirements

Keyue Xing*, Wentao Ding*, Mengmeng Wang, Wenming Tu, Zilong Zheng, Yipeng Kang

Under review, ICASSP 2027 · arXiv · Project page

DuplexAct-Bench tests whether, when, and how full-duplex agents should join a live dialogue, scoring timing and content across six behaviors, 1,290 bilingual trials, and 12 systems.
